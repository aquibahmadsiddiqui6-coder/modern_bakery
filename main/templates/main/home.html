OUR STORY

A tradition that
began in 1960.

Modern Tea & Modern Bakery has been part of Lucknow's bakery culture for generations. Our focus is simple — quality products, familiar flavours and a warm experience.

More than a bakery, Modern is part of Lucknow's everyday rhythm.

Modern began as a humble tea shop, serving warm cups and familiar conversations. Over time, that little tea stop grew into the bakery loved by generations.

Today, you can watch the craft happen in real time with live cake making and live oven features, bringing the freshness and theatre of the bakery right to the counter.
